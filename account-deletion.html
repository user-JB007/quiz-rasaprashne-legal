Quiz-Rasaprashne: delete your account and data

This page is also section 5 of the Quiz-Rasaprashne privacy policy.

Developer: Bhagath J · Contact: helpme.dev@outlook.com · App: Quiz-Rasaprashne (com.rasaprashne.quiz)

In the app

  1. Open Quiz-Rasaprashne and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile circle at the top left of the home screen and choose Account (app version 1.7 and later; in older versions: the Account & privacy card at the bottom of the topic list).
  3. Scroll down to Delete account, tap it, then Delete permanently.
  4. If you signed in a while ago and use Firebase sign-in, the app asks for your password (or a Google sign-in) once more in a "Confirm it's you" dialog. Confirm, and the account is deleted, you are signed out, and the app returns to the sign-in screen. If you cancel, or the deletion fails (for example offline), the app says clearly that nothing was deleted and your account stays as it was.

This deletes your account, your saved best scores, and your profile data (display name, birthday, age confirmation). If you had joined the public leaderboard, your entries are removed from it first. It cannot be undone.

Without the app

  • Local accounts exist only on your phone. Uninstall the app, or clear its data (Android Settings > Apps > Quiz-Rasaprashne > Storage > Clear data).
  • Firebase accounts (only if your version had Firebase sign-in): email helpme.dev@outlook.com from your registered address with the subject "Delete my Quiz-Rasaprashne account". We delete the account and any leaderboard entries within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted: account (email, password hash or Firebase user and linked Google sign-in), best scores, profile data (display name, birthday) and public-leaderboard entries. Nothing else about you is stored by us. Firebase may keep routine security logs for a limited time under Google's retention rules.
