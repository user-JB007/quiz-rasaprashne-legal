1. Summary

Quiz-Rasaprashne is a quiz app. It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We run no server of our own. Most data stays on your phone.

2. Information the app handles

Account details (email and password). To save your scores you create an account with an email address and password.

  • Standard build (local accounts): your email address and a salted, one-way hash of your password (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent to us or anyone else. We cannot recover your password. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the account.
  • If Firebase sign-in is enabled in the version you installed: your email address and password (or your Google account sign-in) are sent to Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your account and processes it under Google's terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy, https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). Firebase may process technical data such as IP address and device identifiers to operate and secure the sign-in service. If you choose Sign in with Google, Google shares your Google account identifier, email address and name with the app.

Profile (optional): display name and birthday. In the Profile screen you can set a display name and a birthday. Both are stored only on your device (in the app's private storage) and are deleted with your account. The birthday is optional and is used only to check that you are 13 or older; it is never sent to us, to Firebase or to any leaderboard. The display name is also stored only on your device unless you switch on the public leaderboard (see below). At sign-up you must also tick a box saying you are 13 or older.

Password reset emails (only if Firebase sign-in is enabled). If you tap Send password reset email (profile circle > Account), Firebase Authentication sends a reset link to your account's email address. Nothing else is sent.

Contacting support. Contact support (profile circle > Support) opens your own email app with a message to helpme.dev@outlook.com that is pre-filled with the app version, your phone model and Android version, to help us answer. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app, and you can edit or remove those lines. We use support emails only to answer you.

Email verification (only if Firebase sign-in is enabled). The Profile screen can ask Firebase to send a verification email to your address and shows whether it has been verified. In the standard build (local accounts) email verification is not available, because sending an email needs an online service; the app says so and does not check your address.

Public leaderboard (only if Firebase is enabled in the version you installed; off by default). The app always shows a leaderboard of the people who played on your own device, built from the scores stored there. If the developer has enabled online accounts, a worldwide leaderboard also exists. You are not on it unless you switch on "Show me on the public leaderboard" (profile circle > Account; in versions before 1.7 it was in Profile). If you do, your display name and best scores (per topic and overall) are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and shown to other signed-in players. Your email address, birthday and account ID are not shown (an internal user ID is used as the record key). You can switch it off at any time, which deletes your entries, and deleting your account deletes them too. Scores are sent by the app and cannot be verified. Please do not use a real full name or anything personal as your display name; names are checked by a simple filter that cannot catch everything.

Scores and settings. Your best scores per topic, your chosen play mode, theme (light/dark), and the online-questions switch are stored only on your device.

Online questions (optional). If the "Online questions" switch is on (it is on by default) and you play Endless mode in a topic that supports it, the app requests extra questions from Open Trivia DB (https://opentdb.com) over HTTPS. The request contains only the question category, the number of questions and a random session token issued by that service. No account details or scores are sent. Like any web request, it exposes your device's IP address to opentdb.com, which has its own privacy practices. You can switch online questions off at any time; the built-in questions then work with no internet connection.

What we do NOT collect: location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, advertising IDs, or analytics/crash data. The app's only permission is internet access (used for online questions and, if enabled, sign-in).

3. How we use information

Only to run the app: to sign you in, to store your scores on your device, and to fetch optional online questions. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. Display names and scores are shown to other players only if you opt in to the public leaderboard.

4. Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone, except: (a) Google, when you use Firebase/Google sign-in, email verification or the public leaderboard (section 2); and (b) opentdb.com receives the technical request described above when online questions are on.

5. How to delete your account and data

You can delete your account and the data linked to it at any time, inside the app or by email request. The same instructions are also published as a separate page: Delete your Quiz-Rasaprashne account and data.

In the app (fastest): open the app, sign in, tap your profile circle (top left of the home screen) > Account > Delete account (version 1.7 and later; older versions: Account & privacy card at the bottom of the topic list) and confirm. The app then deletes:

  • your account (for local accounts: the stored email and password hash on your device; for Firebase accounts: your Firebase Authentication user, including a linked Google sign-in),
  • your saved best scores, display name, birthday and age confirmation on the device, and
  • your entries on the public leaderboard, if you had joined it (they are removed first; if you are offline the app asks you to try again).
    For Firebase accounts, if you signed in a while ago, the app asks you to enter your password (or sign in with Google) once more to confirm it is you, then deletes the account.

Without the app (for example if you uninstalled it or can no longer sign in):

  • Local accounts: nothing is stored anywhere except your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Quiz-Rasaprashne > Storage > Clear data, deletes everything.
  • Firebase accounts (only if the version you used had Firebase sign-in enabled): email helpme.dev@outlook.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my Quiz-Rasaprashne account". We will delete the Firebase user and any public-leaderboard entries within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is kept: nothing about you is kept after deletion by us. Firebase may keep routine security logs for a limited period under Google's own retention rules. Opentdb.com's server logs (IP address of a question request) are outside our control.

5a. Retention

  • Local accounts and scores: kept on your device until you delete the account, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Firebase accounts (if enabled): kept until you delete the account as described above.
  • Backups: the app disables Android cloud backup of its data (allowBackup=false).

6. Security

Passwords in local accounts are never stored in clear text; they are hashed with a per-account random salt. Network requests use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, and local accounts have no password reset.

7. Children

Quiz-Rasaprashne is a general-audience app intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The app asks you to confirm that you are 13 or older when you create an account, and refuses a birthday that shows an age under 13; a birthday showing an age under 13 is not saved and the app tells the person to stop using it and delete the account; accounts of people who answer that they are under 13 in the age check are deleted. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at the address above.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's DPDP Act or the EU/UK GDPR) you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Because local data stays on your device, you can exercise most of these yourself. For anything else, contact helpme.dev@outlook.com.
